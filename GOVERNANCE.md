# Governance

AT Resource Graph is an open project with a maintainer-led review process
during incubation. The maintainer may merge changes that preserve the stated
scope, security boundaries, and compatibility rules.

## Draft namespace

The initial Lexicons use `me.lqdev.resourcegraph.temp`. This namespace is
temporary and controlled as an incubation area. It is not presented as a
neutral or community standard.

## Community Lexicon path

A proposal to move a stable, generic subset into `community.lexicon.*` must
pass a governance gate:

- more than one independent implementation demonstrates interoperability;
- examples, compatibility notes, and conformance fixtures are public;
- the proposal has a public discussion and a named maintenance group;
- the community.lexicon working-group and Technical Steering Committee
  processes accept the proposal.

Until that gate is met, draft schemas may evolve under the temporary namespace.
Consumers must treat them as experimental.
